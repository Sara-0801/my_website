🍪 Butter Cookies 曲奇饼干

Buttery, crisp cookies that are perfect for sharing!

Makes: About 20 cookies

Ingredients

  • 125 g unsalted butter, softened but not melted
  • 60 g icing sugar
  • 1 egg yolk, at room temperature
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 170 g plain flour
  • ¼ teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Beat the softened butter and icing sugar together until smooth and fluffy.
  2. Add the egg yolk and vanilla extract. Mix until combined.
  3. Sift in the flour and salt. Fold gently until a soft dough forms.
  4. Shape the dough into a log about 4 cm wide. Wrap and refrigerate for about 1 hour, until firm enough to slice.
  5. Preheat the oven to 170°C, or 150°C fan. Line a baking tray with baking paper.
  6. Cut the dough into slices about 7–8 mm thick.
  7. Place the slices on the tray, leaving about 2 cm between them.
  8. Bake for 15–18 minutes, until the edges are lightly golden. Check near the end, as ovens vary.
  9. Leave on the tray for 5–10 minutes, then transfer to a cooling rack. The cookies will become crisp as they cool.

Chef's Tip

Mix gently after adding the flour to keep the cookies tender. If the dough softens while slicing, chill it again briefly.
