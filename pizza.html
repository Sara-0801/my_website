🍕 Cheese Pizza

Golden cheese, a crispy base, and a slice worth sharing!

Serves: 2–3

Ingredients

  • 1 ready-made, pre-baked pizza base, about 25 cm wide
  • 100 g grated mozzarella cheese
  • 50 g softened cream cheese

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven according to the pizza base packet instructions.
  2. Place the pizza base on a baking tray.
  3. Spread the cream cheese over the base, leaving a small border.
  4. Sprinkle mozzarella over the top.
  5. Bake according to the base packet instructions, usually about 10–15 minutes, until the base is hot and the cheese is melted and lightly golden.
  6. Let the pizza cool for a few minutes before slicing.

Chef's Tip

Keep the topping layer thin so the pizza base stays crisp.
