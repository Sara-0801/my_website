🍦 Ice-cream Cake

No oven needed. The hardest ingredient is patience!

Serves: 8

Freezing time: At least 6 hours

Ingredients

  • 200 g chocolate sandwich biscuits
  • 50 g melted butter
  • 1 litre vanilla ice cream
  • 500 ml strawberry ice cream
  • Chocolate sauce for serving
  • Sprinkles for decoration

Instructions

  1. Line a 20 cm springform cake tin with baking paper.
  2. Crush the biscuits into fine crumbs.
  3. Mix the crumbs with the melted butter.
  4. Press the mixture firmly into the bottom of the tin.
  5. Freeze the base for 20 minutes.
  6. Let the vanilla ice cream soften just enough to spread.
  7. Spread it over the biscuit base and freeze for 1 hour.
  8. Soften the strawberry ice cream just enough to spread.
  9. Add it on top of the vanilla layer.
  10. Cover and freeze for at least 6 hours, or overnight, until firm.
  11. Remove from the tin and decorate with chocolate sauce and sprinkles.
  12. Let it stand briefly until easy to slice, then serve.

Chef's Tip

Swap strawberry for chocolate ice cream to create a different flavour combination!
